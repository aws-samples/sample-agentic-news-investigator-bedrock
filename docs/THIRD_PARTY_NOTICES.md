# Third-party services and notices

This sample calls a number of external services and depends on one proprietary SDK. Using it
means accepting those providers' terms. Nothing here is redistributed: feeds are read, prices
are fetched, models are invoked; article bodies are kept privately for 30 days for the agent's
own reading and are not republished. Each row records what the provider's terms said when the
authors last reviewed them (2026-10-07). Several publishers' terms restrict automated access or
use with AI systems, and three could not be retrieved; those rows say so plainly. Every source
whose terms prohibit this use, could not be reviewed, or do not address automated use ships
**off** and is opt-in ([OPERATIONS.md § 11](OPERATIONS.md#11-opt-in-data-sources)). Whether your
deployment complies is your responsibility: read the current terms, and disable any source you
cannot use.

## Acknowledgements

The investigator's design — a master model in a bounded loop, specialist tools compressing raw
inputs to text, every decision scored against what happened next, and a replay benchmark for
comparing variants — follows the pattern described in *LongVideoAgent* (arXiv:2512.20618).
The paper is a design influence, not a dependency.

## How to keep this file honest

When you add a data source, a model, or a dependency with a notice obligation, add a row here
in the same commit, with the date you reviewed its terms and what they say. When you remove one,
remove the row. Re-review a row when its provider updates its terms.
